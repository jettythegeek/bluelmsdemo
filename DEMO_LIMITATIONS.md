# Demonstration limitations

BlueLMS NextGen is a static product-vision demonstration, not a deployed school system.

- Every person, family, message, support request, invoice, mark and statistic is fictional demo data.
- Ubakala Smart School and Umuomaikwu Smart School are illustrative deployment contexts only. The demo does not claim they currently use BlueLMS.
- BlueLMS Secondary Demo School is explicitly fictional.
- Navigation, workspace switching, school selection, sidebar collapse, and Primary/Secondary mode switching work in the browser.
- Search, messaging, admissions, attendance recording, payments, exports, downloads, settings, tests and operational buttons do not perform transactions.
- AI responses and management insights are prewritten illustrations. No model or external API is called.
- Offline downloads, sync queues, CBT eligibility and device status are illustrative states. No service worker, offline examination engine or device-management connection is present.
- There is no backend, database, authentication, analytics tracking, secret, or persistence.
- Role-oriented screens demonstrate the intended information boundary; they do not constitute a production security implementation.
- Production architecture, validation, safeguarding, privacy assessment, delivery schedule and commercial terms require a separate approved phase.
