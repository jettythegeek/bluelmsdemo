# 12–15 minute management walkthrough

## 1. Frame the vision — 1 minute

Start on the welcome screen. Explain that BlueLMS connects six perspectives around one trusted school record. Point out the visible product-vision and demo-data labels.

## 2. BGDL Control Centre — 3 minutes

Open **Executive Overview**. Present the portfolio view, then move to **Devices & Connectivity** to discuss classroom tablet allocation and intended offline support. Open **AI Management Insights** and explicitly identify the examples as scripted future capability.

## 3. School Management — 2 minutes

Use the workspace switcher and open **School Overview**. Change the school selector once to demonstrate multi-school context. Review **Assets & Maintenance** to show operational readiness.

## 4. Teacher Studio — 2 minutes

Open **Teacher Dashboard**, then **Lesson Planner**. Show the flow from today’s teaching schedule to a structured lesson. Open **AI Teaching Assistant** and emphasise teacher review and control.

## 5. Student Learning — 3 minutes

Open **Learning Home**, then **Lesson Viewer**. Switch between Primary and Secondary modes to show age-appropriate presentation. Open **Offline Downloads** and explain that the screen communicates the planned experience, not an active sync engine.

## 6. Parent Connect — 2 minutes

Open **Family Overview**, then **Academic Progress** or **Attendance**. Emphasise linked children, simple language and clear, family-friendly learning context.

## 7. Stakeholder Intelligence and close — 2 minutes

Open **Oversight Overview**, then **Learning Outcomes**. Point out that this workspace intentionally contains aggregate information and no private pupil records. Close with the proposition: management is approving the direction and validation phase, not accepting a finished production platform.

## Presenter safeguards

- Use the provided routes in this order and avoid improvising operational actions.
- Keep a built `dist/` copy available locally; no internet connection is required for core rendering.
- If asked whether a control works, say: “This prototype validates the experience. Production workflows follow technical and safeguarding validation.”
- Use the in-app **59 screens** button when management asks about breadth.
